# Social Media Management Skill

## Capabilities
- Manage content calendars.
- Generate posts and captions.
- Create hashtags.
- Prepare images and videos.
- Schedule social media posts.
- Monitor engagement.
- Generate performance reports.
- Manage supported social accounts.

## Platforms
- Facebook
- Instagram
- TikTok
- X
- LinkedIn

## Safety
- Require approval before publishing.
- Protect account credentials.
- Never send spam.
- Respect platform policies.
