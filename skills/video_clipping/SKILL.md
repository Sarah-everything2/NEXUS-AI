\# NEXUS AI Video Clipping Skill



\## Purpose

Automatically transform long videos into engaging short-form content.



\## Capabilities

1\. Detect interesting moments.

2\. Extract short video clips.

3\. Add subtitles and captions.

4\. Resize videos for TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

5\. Generate titles, descriptions and hashtags.

6\. Prepare clips for publishing.

7\. Organise finished videos.



\## Planned Integrations

\- FFmpeg

\- AI transcription

\- Speech recognition

\- Video editing tools

\- Social media platforms



\## Workflow

1\. Receive a video.

2\. Analyse its content.

3\. Identify interesting segments.

4\. Extract selected clips.

5\. Generate captions.

6\. Export finished videos.



\## Safety

\- Never publish without approval.

\- Respect copyright.

\- Never overwrite original videos.

\- Keep original files safe.
