# YouTube Automation Skill

## Capabilities
- Research video topics and trends.
- Generate video scripts.
- Create titles and descriptions.
- Generate thumbnails.
- Prepare videos for upload.
- Schedule publishing.
- Analyse channel performance.
- Track views, subscribers and engagement.

## Safety
- Obtain approval before publishing.
- Never misrepresent content.
- Protect channel credentials.
- Respect copyright and platform policies.
