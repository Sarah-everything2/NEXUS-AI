# AI-to-Earn Skill

## Capabilities
- Discover AI-related earning opportunities.
- Research legitimate platforms.
- Identify freelance opportunities.
- Discover AI training and contribution projects.
- Track applications and tasks.
- Monitor payments and earnings.

- Never promise guaranteed income.
- Identify fees and financial risks.
- Avoid fraudulent opportunities.
- Require approval for payments.
- Protect personal and financial information.
